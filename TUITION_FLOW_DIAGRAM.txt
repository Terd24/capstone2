╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                              EXAMPLE SCENARIO                                ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  OWNER:
    Sets Grade 11 STEM tuition = ₱54,000 for 2024-2025

  REGISTRAR:
    Adds 3 students:
    • John Doe - Grade 11 STEM
    • Jane Smith - Grade 11 STEM
    • Bob Lee - Grade 11 STEM

  SYSTEM:
    Automatically creates 3 fee records:
    • John Doe: ₱54,000 tuition (unpaid)
    • Jane Smith: ₱54,000 tuition (unpaid)
    • Bob Lee: ₱54,000 tuition (unpaid)

  CASHIER:
    Day 1: John pays ₱20,000 → Balance: ₱34,000
    Day 5: Jane pays ₱54,000 → Balance: ₱0 (PAID IN FULL)
    Day 10: John pays ₱34,000 → Balance: ₱0 (PAID IN FULL)
    Day 15: Bob pays ₱10,000 → Balance: ₱44,000

  RESULT:
    • 2 students fully paid
    • 1 student with ₱44,000 remaining
    • All transactions tracked
    • Owner can update price for next year
