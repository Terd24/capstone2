1. tuition_fee_structure (EXISTING)
   - Stores fee prices set by Owner
   - Columns: grade_level, academic_track, tuition_fee, other_fees, total_fee, school_year

2. student_fee_items (EXISTING)
   - Stores individual student fee records
   - Auto-populated when registrar adds student
   - Columns: id_number, school_year_term, fee_type, amount, paid

3. student_payments (EXISTING)
   - Stores payment transactions
   - Created when cashier records payment
   - Columns: id_number, school_year_term, fee_type, amount, payment_method, date
